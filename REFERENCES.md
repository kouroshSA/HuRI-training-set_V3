# References

## HuRI (Human Reference Interactome)

The starting positive interactome (`data/raw/huri_interactome_uniprotID_seqs.csv.xz`)
is derived from the HuRI project's binary protein-protein interaction data.

> Luck, K., Kim, D.-K., Lambourne, L., et al. "A reference map of the human
> binary protein interactome." *Nature* **580**, 402–408 (2020).
> https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2188-x

Canonical dataset access: the Interactome Atlas portal,
https://www.interactome-atlas.org/ (downloads at
https://www.interactome-atlas.org/download), maintained by CCSB / Dana-Farber
Cancer Institute. Data there is provided under CC BY 4.0.
